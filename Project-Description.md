# Gymix – Project Description

## 1. Introduction

Gymix is a web-based Gym Management System designed to help gyms manage their daily operations efficiently.

The system provides an organized platform for administrators, coaches, and gym members, allowing each user to access features according to their role and permissions.

## 2. Project Objectives

The main objectives of Gymix are:

- Simplify gym management operations.
- Manage gym members and coaches.
- Organize subscription plans and membership details.
- Manage training sessions and schedules.
- Allow members to book and cancel training sessions.
- Track attendance and missed sessions.
- Manage free and paid training session benefits.
- Improve communication between coaches and administrators.

## 3. User Roles

### 3.1 Administrator

The administrator has full control over the system and is responsible for:

- Managing gym members and coaches.
- Creating and managing subscription plans.
- Managing training sessions and schedules.
- Setting session capacity and fees.
- Tracking attendance.
- Managing membership benefits and free sessions.
- Reviewing and responding to coach requests.

### 3.2 Gym Member

Gym members can:

- View their personal information and member ID.
- View their subscription plan and expiration date.
- Browse available training sessions and coaches.
- Book and cancel training sessions.
- View their booking history.
- Track attended, cancelled, missed, and upcoming sessions.
- View remaining free sessions and additional session fees.

### 3.3 Coach

Coaches have access to a read-only dashboard where they can:

- View their personal profile.
- View assigned training sessions.
- View their schedules.
- View members registered in their sessions.
- View attendance records.
- Send requests or messages to the administrator.
- Track the status of their requests.

Coaches cannot modify their profiles, schedules, sessions, members, or attendance records. All changes must be handled by the administrator.

## 4. Subscription Plans

The system supports three main subscription types:

- Monthly Subscription
- Six-Month Subscription
- Annual Subscription

Each subscription plan may include specific benefits, such as a limited number of free training sessions.

The system tracks the number of free sessions used and the remaining balance. If a member exceeds the included free sessions, additional fees may apply.

## 5. Main System Features

- Authentication and Role-Based Access Control
- Member Management
- Coach Management
- Subscription Management
- Training Session Management
- Schedule and Capacity Management
- Session Booking and Cancellation
- Attendance Tracking
- Free and Paid Session Tracking
- Coach Requests Management

## 6. Technologies

- Frontend: React.js
- Backend: Node.js and ASP.NET Core
- Database: To be determined by the development team.

## 7. Project Scope

Gymix focuses on providing the essential tools needed to manage gym operations through a web-based application.

The project will be developed by a team of four members within an estimated period of 30 days.
